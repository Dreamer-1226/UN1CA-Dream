Privileged diagnostic investigation, 2026-11-06

Build the root-only helper (does not push or run anything):
  target/dm3q/camera/diagnostics/portrait-pair-probe/build-privileged.sh

PrivilegedProbe.java contains two diagnostic entry paths:
1. main: temporary root app_process runner, with explicit optional Samsung Camera
   UID/context and validated real-PID forwarding experiments. These experiments
   are NOT a working capture route on the tested ROM. Root alone is rejected by
   Samsung's package policy. Running as the Camera UID resolves that gate, but
   the standalone process is still rejected during permission checking. Forwarding
   to the real Camera PID is also rejected as untrusted. These modes must not be
   described as successful pair tests.
2. runInApplication(Context): entry intended to execute inside the real Samsung
   Camera process, using its own application context and UID. It inventories20,
   54 and52, then requests bounded physical YUV streams for up to15 seconds.
   It never calls System.exit or changes UIDs in this entry. Results are logged
   under tag PortraitPairPrivileged. It closes its session/readers and stops its
   own HandlerThread. This entry compiled successfully; it has NOT executed on
   the phone because the runtime attachment failed before loading the helper.

Do not call main inside Samsung Camera: main is for a standalone app_process
and can terminate its own process. In-app diagnostics must use runInApplication.
Do not infer calibration validity from the existence of the760-byte files.
Do not change camera-service allowlists, disable SELinux, rewrite calibration
headers, replace the installed Camera APK, or fake available vendor tags to
make a probe report success.

Observed on the connected S23U:
- Privileged characteristics access works. Logical20 contains56,58,52,54.
- Physical54 advertises27.2mm focal length;52 advertises7.9mm.
- Explicit lookup of dualCalibrationT_W and dualCalibrationT2_T1 reports that
  the tags do not exist. Older dualCalibration on20 is760 bytes.
- The zoom-translator's device T1T2 and WT1 dumps are760 bytes, differ from
  the bundled calibration, and have byte0x2f3=0 instead of fallback's4.
  These are promising real-calibration artifacts; pair transform direction,
  per-unit provenance and Portrait-engine acceptance remain unvalidated.
- No requested pair image buffers or depth results were obtained.
